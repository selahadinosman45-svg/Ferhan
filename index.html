ferhan learning academy

A structured six-stage path from Arabic letters to confident Qur’an revision.

0 of 6 stages complete
01

Alphabet & articulation

Recognise all Arabic letters, their names, shapes and basic articulation points. Practise similar sounds slowly.

02

Harakat & letter combinations

Learn fatḥah, kasrah, ḍammah, tanwīn, sukūn, shaddah, and how letters join.

03

Reading fluency

Blend syllables, read short words and phrases, and practise daily with a teacher checking pronunciation.

04

Tajweed foundations

Study makhārij, ṣifāt, madd, ghunnah, qalqalah, nūn sākinah and stopping/starting. Apply rules with teacher feedback.

05

Surah-by-surah study

Begin with Al-Fātiḥah. Listen, read the words, review meanings, then progress to short surahs.

06

Memorisation & revision

Use short daily portions, spaced revision, repeat settings and regular recitation checks with a qualified teacher.

Stage completion is a personal study tracker, not a certification of reading or Tajweed ability. Ask a qualified teacher to assess your recitation before moving to advanced material.
